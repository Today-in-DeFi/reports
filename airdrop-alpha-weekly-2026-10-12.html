$1.2B Protocol Just Dropped Its Token — Are You Eligible?

Actions

  1. Lead with a single claimable event, not two. The Oct 7 headline split attention between Concrete and Fables. 59% of clicks went to Concrete — it should have been the entire headline.
  2. Use "eligibility" and "claim window" language. These create personal stakes ("do I qualify?") and time pressure. "TGE moves to Oct 20" is informational; "Claim window closes Oct 20" is actionable.
  3. Use asymmetry framing in tweets. "Minimum, not a cap" produced the best tweet of the week. The formula: state the known floor + imply the unknown ceiling.
  4. Add a clear "Who qualifies" subsection. 59% of clicks to the claim page means readers are ready to act — reduce friction by answering eligibility in the letter itself.
  5. Add a cross-link to Farming Alpha. Airdrop clickers are high-intent DeFi users who'd likely engage with yield content. One line near the CTA.
  6. Track OR against 20.84% baseline. This issue needs a meaningful recovery — test single-focus anticipation framing next.
