Actions for the Next LWID

  1. Lead with the week's highest-consequence loss, exploit, contradiction, or unresolved risk.
  2. Keep the headline within 55 characters and move the second story into preview text when needed.
  3. Use one narrative tweet: event → unexpected response → unresolved question.
  4. Use one knowledge-gap tweet that withholds the mechanism rather than the entire conclusion.
  5. Move lower-priority items into “Worth watching” and target fewer than 3,000 words.
  6. Use the highest-click topic as the end-of-letter cross-link or premium CTA.
