Key Findings

1. Protocol tweet links dominate. Readers use the LWID to discover what happened, then click through to the source tweet to verify. The top-clicked links across all 4 issues are almost all x.com/protocol tweets. Every item should link to the source announcement.
2. Security/exploit items get the most clicks. PeckShield alerts, SlowMist reports, ColdCard disclosure, Allbridge exploit — these consistently get 23–35 clicks each. The Issues section is the LWID’s most engaged section, not DeFi launches. Rank Issues first, not last.
3. /subscribe gets 26–27 clicks on the best issues. The LWID is a subscriber acquisition channel. The best issues (Aug 3, Jul 20) drove 26–27 subscribe clicks vs near-zero on the worst (Aug 10). High-engagement content converts.
4. todayindefi.com articles outperform when included. The CLARITY Act article on todayindefi.com got 35 clicks — tied for the highest-clicked link across all 4 weeks. Linking to your own site (not just tweets) drives owned traffic. Currently rare in the LWID.
5. Aug 10 underperformed on every metric. Lowest OR (19.30%), lowest clicks (37), lowest CTR (4%). The issue led with announcements (Circle, Ether.fi) instead of threats. Top link was only 17 clicks vs 35 on better issues. Announcement-led issues drive less engagement than threat-led issues across both opens AND clicks.
