Headline was the problem, not distribution. The 3.88pp gap is the widest between consecutive Guide issues. Mechanism transparency wins. The Guide audience values mechanism transparency above all. "Here's How it Works" (21.47%) and "Here's How" (20.78%) both beat the baseline. The audience opens the Guide to learn a process, not to discover a yield number — that's what Farming Alpha is for.

Recovery Plan

  1. Use a well-known protocol with existing reader interest. The Uniswap Guide (21.47%) benefited from brand recognition. Picking a protocol readers already use reduces the "is this worth my time?" barrier. Good candidates: Aave, Pendle, Hyperliquid, Morpho.
  2. Use "Here's How" or "Step-by-Step" in the headline. Two of the three best-performing Guides used this framing. It promises concrete, actionable information — the Guide's core value proposition.
  3. Use numbered scope when appropriate. "5 DeFi Tools" (20.36%) sets expectations and signals completeness. "3 Ways to Use [Protocol]" or "5 Things You Can Do on [Protocol]" are testable variants.
  4. Avoid extreme yield claims without mechanism context. "2 to 3-digit APRs" (17.59%) created credibility friction. If the yield is genuinely high, explain why in the headline: "Arc Pays 40% — Here's the Mechanism and the Risk."
  5. Target 2,000–2,500 words. The Oct 2 issue was too short (1,392) for the Guide format. Readers expect depth — walkthroughs, risk analysis, and step-by-step instructions.
  6. Re-establish the Friday cadence. Two consecutive weeks without a Guide weakens the publishing habit. Even a shorter, simpler Guide is better than a gap.
