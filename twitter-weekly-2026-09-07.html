Best performer: “$111M in tokenized stocks” tweet (4.10% ER, 14 clicks). Surprising fact (“$111M”) + exclusivity (“most of the market hasn’t noticed yet”) + clear CTA. This was a Monday tweet promoting the previous Friday’s Guide — confirming the pattern that Monday follow-up tweets outperform.
Best click-driver: “$66M gone in 20 minutes” (16 clicks). Fear hooks continue to drive the most link clicks. The specific dollar amount + timeframe creates urgency.
Simple yield pitches underperform again. “Farm >10% stablecoin APR on Morpho Vaults” (765 imp, 1.31% ER) and “A stable ETH pool paying >10% APR on Uniswap” (743 imp, 1.21% ER) are the weakest tweets. No context, no tension, no reason to click beyond the number.
Thursday had the best engagement day (3,990 imp, 0.98% ER). The Guide promo tweet “Stablecoin loops just got lower risk” (1,096 imp, 3.10% ER) led the day — it used mechanism framing with a clear benefit rather than just quoting a yield number.
