Actions

  1. Frame headlines as cause-and-effect transformations, not price changes. "FLUID Ran 27%" closes the loop. "Jupiter Lend Hit $1.3B — One Token Gained 27% From It" opens it. The reader needs to feel they'll learn something they can't get from the price chart.
  2. Use "hit $X" or "crossed $X" instead of "grew." Concrete milestones create a before/after contrast. "Grew" is directional but unmemorable.
  3. Test institutional-bridge headlines. The Visa/Mastercard stablecoin tweet had the highest CTR (0.67%) despite lowest reach. "Visa and Mastercard Just Agreed on One Stablecoin" is a testable Analysis headline.
  4. Add a "yield of the week" callout box. Click data shows Analysis readers engage with yield content (@yield led clicks). Use this to cross-link to Farming Alpha.
  5. The four-week OR decline needs a headline formula reset. Four consecutive drops from 21.12% to 19.22% is structural, not noise. Next issue should test contradiction or institutional framing.
  6. Track OR against 20.06% baseline and direct-tweet CTR above 0.5%.
