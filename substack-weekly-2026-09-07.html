Headline Analysis

Tuesday & Wednesday headlines worked well. Both used curiosity-driven, contrarian framing with specific numbers. “$4.8M TVL DEX Did $6B in Volume” creates intrigue through an absurd ratio. “This New DEX is Generating $2B Volume and Has No Token Yet” signals opportunity. Both hit 20%+ OR.
Thursday & Friday headlines fell flat again. Both are product-listing format: “Farm >10% on Morpho & >10% ETH APY on Uniswap” and “Earn 11% Fixed APR From Funding Rates Arbitrage.” No tension, no context, no “why now.” Farming has now hit sub-19% in 4 of the last 5 issues. The Guide hit 18.4% for the second consecutive week.
Suggested rewrites:
Thu: “Lending Pays 4%. Two Vaults Still Pay 10%+.” (context + contrast)
Fri: “Funding Rates Flip Overnight — One Protocol Lets You Lock Them” (tension + curiosity)

Key Takeaways

Farming Alpha has now been sub-19% in 4 of the last 5 issues. This week’s 18.3% continues the longest decline of any TID letter. The product-listing headline format (“Farm X% on [Protocol]”) is exhausted. The one exception in 5 weeks was “Base Rates Are Under 5% — 2 Farms Still Pay 11–19%” which hit 21.6%.
Guide hit 18.4% for the second consecutive week. “Earn 11% Fixed APR From Funding Rates Arbitrage” is another yield-listing headline with no tension or curiosity. Two weeks in a row at the bottom.
Tue/Wed headlines show the path forward. Both used curiosity + specific numbers and cleared 20%. The pattern: name a surprising fact, imply an opportunity, let the reader wonder “how?” Apply this same framing to Thu/Fri.
Low-opening letters drive the highest engagement. Thu (6% CTR) and Fri (6% CTR) had the worst OR but best click-through — proving the audience that opens these letters is deeply engaged. Fix the headline, keep the content.
