Net followers went negative. 8 new follows vs 11 unfollows = -3 net. First negative week in the tracking period. Sunday saw 3 unfollows on a day with zero posts.
Airdrops roundup was the engagement winner. 934 imp, 2.5% ER, 23 engagements. The urgency framing (“deadline ending soon,” “20x multipliers now live”) drives action. This format should appear 2x/week.
The “covered puts” angle was diluted. Three tweets on Jul 24 and Jul 27 covered the same DeFi options/yield angle (554 + 688 + 664 imp). A single consolidated thread would likely have broken 1,500+. One tweet per angle, always.
No breakout tweet this week. Top post was 1,264 imp (recap). Last week had the Robinhood Chain map at 3,732. The account needs at least one “anchor” post per week — a guide, map, or explainer — to keep the overall reach floor above 15K.
Sponsored disclosure format needs rethinking. The bare “proudly supported by” post (124 imp) tanks reach and likely signals “ad” to the algorithm. Merge the disclosure into the value tweet instead of posting it separately.
