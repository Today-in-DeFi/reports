Key Takeaways

Open rates declined for the third consecutive week: 20.6% → 19.8% → 19.4%. Friday’s guide (18.4%) set a new 2026 low for any individual letter.
Wednesday’s Airdrop letter was the 2nd-best performer (20.6% OR). However, its click-through rate was the lowest of any letter (3%) — readers open for FOMO but aren’t clicking claim links. Link directly to claim pages, not protocol homepages.
Monday LWID remains the anchor — 20.5% OR and 7% click-through rate. Threat-first headlines (“Term Finance Lost $8.5M”) consistently outperform yield listings.
Friday’s guide was the most valuable letter for the sponsor — 34 DeFi Saver clicks (4% of openers) vs 15–19 on other days. Low opens but highest per-reader engagement and sponsor value.
Thu/Fri headlines need tension framing. Compare: “Base Rates Are Under 5% — 2 Farms Still Pay 11–19%” (21.6% OR, two weeks ago) vs “Balancer Pools Pay 11% Stable APY” (18.5% this week). Same content, 3pp difference.
