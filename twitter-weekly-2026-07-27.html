Key Takeaways

URL clicks halved again (68 → 31). No single tweet drove meaningful traffic this week. Last week’s Robinhood Chain map (33 clicks alone) carried that metric. Without an “anchor” piece, clicks collapse.
Net followers went negative for the first time (-3). 8 new follows vs 11 unfollows. Sunday saw 3 unfollows on a day with zero posts — inactivity + low-value content earlier in the week compounds.
Airdrops roundup = highest engagement rate (2.5%). Urgency framing works (“deadline ending soon,” “20x multipliers now live”). This is the only format consistently driving both reach and engagement. Should appear 2x/week.
The covered-puts angle was diluted across 3 tweets. Three variations of the same options-yield concept on Jul 24+27 (554, 688, 664 imp). One consolidated tweet would have likely broken 1,500. Rule: one tweet per angle.
No breakout tweet this week. Highest was 1,264 (recap). Last week had the Robinhood map at 3,732. The account needs one guide/map/explainer early in the week to establish a reach floor.
Sponsored disclosure tanked reach. The bare “proudly supported by” post (124 imp) likely signals “ad” to the algorithm. Merge disclosure into the value tweet next time.
