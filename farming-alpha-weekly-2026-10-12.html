Actions

  1. Reset the Farming headline formula. Two consecutive sub-20% issues demand it. Stop using "Earn" and "Farm" as lead verbs. Use "Lock" when the rate is durable, or use count + range ("3 Farms Paying 7–12%") for mixed sets.
  2. Name at least one protocol or chain in the headline. "Lock 12% on Pendle" > "Earn 12% Stable LP APR." The Pendle pool got 5 direct clicks; the headline should have featured it.
  3. Add explicit risk labels per farm. Sep 24's "Low Risk" headline hit 20.92%. Oct 8's partial risk coverage coincided with the lowest OR. Make risk a first-class element: add a summary table with Protocol | Rate | Risk Level | Link at the top.
  4. Add "details and risks" to tweet CTAs. Sep 24's tweet set used this framing and drove 16 clicks (vs Oct 8's 3). The reader needs to know they'll learn the risk profile, not just the number.
  5. Test the named-chain tweet format. The Oct 1 Monad tweet (1,030 imp, 8 clicks, 2.62% ER) is the proof case. Name the chain, state the rate, add the incentive angle.
  6. Next issue must recover above 20% to prevent baseline erosion. Use "Lock" + named protocol + risk cue.
