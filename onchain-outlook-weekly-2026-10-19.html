Actions for the Next Onchain Outlook

  1. Use specific entity comparisons, not broad categories. “Hyperliquid’s USDC Pile Now Tops Solana’s” (20.57%) outperformed “DeFi Tokens Outperformed Bitcoin by 20%” (19.06%) by 1.51pp. Name the protocols.
  2. Don’t close the curiosity loop in the headline. “FLUID Ran 27%” tells the full story. “One Token Rallied 27% — Here’s Why” withholds the name and drives opens.
  3. Embed analyst/thought-leader tweets, not just protocol tweets. The OO audience clicks on people with opinions (@AriDavidPaul 9 clicks, @VitalikButerin 4, @andrewcuomo 5). Include at least one analyst thread per issue.
  4. Reduce DeFi Llama reference links. Oct 13 had 8 DeFi Llama clicks but only 8 total clickers — readers used the letter as a data dashboard, not a narrative. Use DeFi Llama data in your analysis but link to the source tweets instead.
  5. Replicate the Derive tweet formula for OO tweets. “Volume rose 6x to $112M — the reason why” got 6,834 impressions. But pair it with a high-CTR tweet (TradFi bridge or name-drop + withhold) to convert reach into clicks.
