5. Action Items

1. Keep the suffix dropped. “- Weekly Recap” is confirmed dead weight. Aug 17 and Aug 24 both fit within inbox preview and both outperformed. Never bring it back.
2. Standardize the summary section as “TL;DR”. Four different names in four weeks. TL;DR is the most recognized format — use it every week, always as the first thing after the subscribe boilerplate.
3. Lead tweets with one dramatic story, not a thread of all issues. The single-story format (“$8.5M gone. No exploit.”) drove 5 URL clicks; the “Issues ⚠️” list format drove zero. Pick the week’s most dramatic incident and make it the lead tweet with a newsletter link.
4. Add a premium CTA to the LWID closing. Zero of the last 7 LWIDs have one. The LWID has the highest OR and highest CTR — it’s the best letter to convert free readers.
5. Add cross-links to Tuesday’s Outlook and Thursday’s Farming. “Want the full onchain breakdown? Tuesday’s Outlook covers the flows.” This connects the content stack and trains readers to open multiple letters.
6. Target 50 chars or fewer for headlines. The two best performers (Aug 17: 55ch, Aug 24: 51ch) both fit or nearly fit. Shorter = fully visible = higher OR.
